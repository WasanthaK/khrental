Scope and contact

This notice explains how personal information is used in Quixo Homes public browsing, accounts, rental enquiries and property management. Quixo, Inc. provides the software. Property owners and managers also handle information for their own rental activities. For privacy questions, contact support@quixo.online and identify Quixo Homes and the relevant organization.

Information and purposes

Account and contact details, verification status and organization memberships
Sign-in, account verification, organization selection and authorized access.
Property details, locations, approved photos and public-listing preferences
Property management and the public listing of selected information. Public listing information can be seen by people who are not signed in.
Enquiries, replies, viewing requests, applications and offers
Communication and the rental process for the selected property.
Agreement details, signatory information, signed documents and signature evidence
Preparing, signing, retaining and retrieving agreements and related evidence.
Rental invoices, recorded payment/deposit references, receipts and settlement records
The organization's rental administration. A recorded payment reference is not proof that Quixo collected or independently verified a transfer.
Maintenance requests, attachments, assignments and comments
Reporting and coordinating maintenance with authorized participants. Staff-only notes are distinct from comments visible to renters.
Management-subscription identifiers, invoice/payment status and selected payment-method summary
Organization subscription administration and reconciliation with Stripe. Enter payment credentials only in the hosted payment flow.
Service, notification and security-event information
Operating the service, notifying participants, investigating errors and protecting account access.

Only provide information needed for the task. Avoid putting unrelated sensitive information into free-text enquiries, maintenance comments or public property descriptions.

Sharing and access

Public listing details are available publicly. Enquiry and application information is shared with the relevant property organization according to the form's sharing notice. Rental records are available only through the applicable membership, participant and permission checks; membership alone does not give every staff member access to every record. Assigned maintenance staff and renters have different access, including restrictions on private comments.

Authorized renters can retrieve supported signed-agreement records. Administrative audit evidence is restricted separately and is not automatically available to everyone who can view the signed agreement.

We use service providers for payments, electronic signing, email delivery, hosting and storage. Information used in these activities includes subscription and payment information, agreement and signatory details, email messages and stored rental records, as relevant to the service being provided.

Retention and requests

Ending a tenancy or a management subscription does not automatically erase its records. Retention depends on the type of information, whether it is needed to provide your account or administer a tenancy, and whether it is needed for accounting, a dispute or an applicable record-keeping obligation. Signed agreements and other retained rental records remain subject to authorized access. Contact us to ask about the retention of a particular record.

You can contact support to request access to, correction of or deletion of your information, or ask about an earlier sharing consent. We will verify the request and identify the responsible organization where needed. Deleting an account, withdrawing a consent, cancelling a subscription and deleting a rental record are different requests. Do not assume one automatically completes the others or removes records already shared with an owner.
