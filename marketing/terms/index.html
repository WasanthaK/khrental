About Quixo Homes

Quixo Homes is software from Quixo, Inc. for finding rental properties and managing rental activity. Property owners and managers supply listings, respond to enquiries and manage their rental agreements. Browsing a listing or sending an enquiry does not reserve a property, confirm a viewing or create a tenancy. Review the owner's response, any rental offer and the agreement before proceeding.

Your rental agreement identifies its parties and rental obligations. A Quixo Homes software subscription is separate from that agreement and from rent, deposits, utilities and other amounts payable to the property owner or manager.

Accounts and organization access

Use accurate account information and keep your sign-in details private. A single account may have access to more than one organization. Choose the appropriate organization before working with its properties or records. Your permissions depend on your verified membership and rental relationship; an account in one organization does not grant access to another organization's records.

Owners and managers are responsible for the information they publish, the people they authorize and the rental terms they offer. Do not publish private renter documents or information you are not entitled to share. Only use the service for lawful rental and property-management activities.

Listings, enquiries and signing

Public browsing is free. Standard public listing carries no separate listing fee and is included for eligible managed properties unless the owner opts out. Availability can change. Request a viewing through the available enquiry controls and wait for confirmation.

When you submit an enquiry or rental application, the form explains which information will be shared with the relevant owner or manager. Read that explanation before submitting. Sending a message or application is separate from accepting a rental offer or signing an agreement. Review the prepared agreement and recipient details before sending it for signature. Each participant must complete their own signing steps.

Management subscriptions

The subscribing customer is the property-management organization. Subscriptions are monthly, with no trial. Your subscription renews automatically each month until cancelled. The chargeable unit is an independently rentable managed dwelling. Vacant or unlisted dwellings still count while managed. A building and its separately rentable units are not counted twice. We show the applicable price, currency, dwelling quantity, taxes or additional charges, and renewal information before you confirm your subscription. Review the cost and effective date before confirming a quantity change.

Adding managed dwellings during a billing period creates a prorated adjustment collected on the next regular invoice. An approved reduction takes effect at the next renewal; the current period remains billed at its existing quantity, with no current-period refund for that reduction. Hiding a listing or marking a property inactive does not itself remove it from the billed inventory.

You can request cancellation at the end of the current subscription period through the available billing controls. Check the resulting renewal status. Cancellation of the software subscription does not cancel a tenancy or delete historical rental records. It does not, by itself, determine a separate refund request.

If a renewal payment fails, you have seven days from the first failed renewal to resolve payment before new paid property-management actions are restricted. Repeated payment failures do not restart that period. Update your payment method through the billing controls and check your subscription status. Payment failure does not delete historical records or remove existing renters' authorized access to their records.

Records and support

Closing a tenancy or cancelling a software subscription is different from deleting records. Signed agreements, retained financial records and authorized renter access are preserved through the supported closure and subscription lifecycle. Access remains subject to the person's verified role and relationship. Signed-document access and administrative signature-audit access are distinct.

For help with your account, subscription, records or a privacy request, contact support@quixo.online. Do not email passwords, payment-card details or identity-document copies with an initial request. We may need to verify your identity and clarify which organization and records your request concerns.
